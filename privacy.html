AURORA CANADA

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Aurora Canada is built to need as little of your information as possible. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Your location

Only if you choose My location. Your position is rounded to about 1 km and used to get the forecast for that spot: the rounded coordinates are sent to Environment Canada’s weather service to fetch cloud data. We don’t run any server that receives or stores your location.

Kept on your phone

Your chosen place, saved places, settings, recent forecasts (for offline use) and any sightings you post stay on your phone. Aurora alerts are worked out on your phone too. Deleting the app removes all of it. You can delete your sightings in Settings.

Sightings

A sighting is saved as an approximate area (about 10 km), never your exact spot, together with the forecast at that moment. Sightings aren’t shared with anyone. If sharing is added later, it will be your choice and this policy will be updated first.

Forecast and map services

Forecasts come from Environment and Climate Change Canada and the U.S. NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. Maps come from Apple on iPhone and Google on Android. Like any website visit, these services see each request (for example your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

Purchases and tips

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details, only confirmation of what was bought so the app can unlock it.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. We don’t sell or share personal information.
