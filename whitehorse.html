Yukon

Whitehorse northern lights forecast

Capital of Yukon, on the Yukon River, surrounded by boreal forest and mountains. It sits near the southern edge of the auroral oval, so local weather and aurora activity both matter.

When is it dark enough?

Aurora is only visible when the sky is dark. In Whitehorse, nights are dark enough (the sun more than 12° below the horizon for at least 3 hours) from about 11 August to 30 April. Around 21 December there are about 14.5 hours of darkness.

Calculated from the sun’s position at this latitude. Aurora also needs clear skies and enough activity, which change night to night.

What the app tells you

For Whitehorse, the app combines the next hour of aurora activity, Environment Canada’s cloud forecast, darkness and moonlight into one plain answer: is it worth going outside right now? It never promises a sighting or a time.
