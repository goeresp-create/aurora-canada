Inuvik northern lights forecast

In the Mackenzie Delta of the Northwest Territories, above the Arctic Circle. Its long polar nights give deep darkness, though during quiet periods the aurora can sit to the south.

When is it dark enough?

Aurora needs a dark sky. In Inuvik, nights are dark enough (the sun at least 12° below the horizon for 3 hours or more) from about 1 September to 9 April. Around 21 December there are about 16 hours of darkness.

Calculated from the sun’s position at this latitude. Clear skies and aurora activity change night to night.

What the app tells you

For Inuvik, the app combines next-hour aurora activity, Environment Canada’s cloud forecast, darkness and moonlight into one answer: is it worth going outside now? It never promises a sighting or a time.
