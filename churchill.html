Churchill northern lights forecast

On the western shore of Hudson Bay in northern Manitoba, reached by air or rail. It lies close to the auroral oval and has the longest dark season of these four places.

When is it dark enough?

Aurora is only visible when the sky is dark. In Churchill, nights are dark enough (the sun more than 12° below the horizon for at least 3 hours) from about 5 August to 6 May. Around 21 December there are about 14 hours of darkness.

Calculated from the sun’s position at this latitude. Aurora also needs clear skies and enough activity, which change night to night.

What the app tells you

For Churchill, the app combines the next hour of aurora activity, Environment Canada’s cloud forecast, darkness and moonlight into one plain answer: is it worth going outside right now? It never promises a sighting or a time.
